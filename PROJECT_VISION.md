## 👥 Fixed Staff Hierarchy: Bhakti 5-Tier Lineage Protocol

The **Bhakti Protocol Hierarchy** is the foundational, multi-layered structural law scaling our enterprise infrastructure. Every entity and task in the system carries strict hierarchical ID lineage:

1. **Level 5: Managing Director (`MD-01` / `EMP-001` — Arslan Malik Bashir Ahmad):**
   - Sovereign executive engine holding capital allocation, escrow oversight, statutory signing, and system bypass authority.
   - Primary active human actor driving enterprise execution.
2. **Level 4: 12 Corporate Departments (`DEPT-01` to `DEPT-12`):**
   - Headed by **12 Department Directors** (`DIR-01` to `DIR-12`).
   - Each Director oversees their strategic operational domain.
3. **Level 3: 9 Sub-Departments per Department (`SUB-01` to `SUB-09`):**
   - Headed by **9 Managers** (`MGR-01` to `MGR-09`) per department.
   - Each Director directly commands 9 Department Managers.
4. **Level 2: 12 Squads per Sub-Department (`SQD-01` to `SQD-12`):**
   - Headed by Squad Leaders titled **Supervisors** (`SUP-01` to `SUP-12`).
   - Each Supervisor commands 1 or more operational Teams.
5. **Level 1: Teams & Modular Tasks (`TSK-001+` & `T-xxx`):**
   - Tasks are technically independent modules tagged with lineage labels (`deptId`, `directorId`, `subDeptId`, `managerId`, `squadId`, `supervisorId`, `teamId`).
   - Tasks not yet assigned to an active squad reside in the **Unassigned Task Pool** (`isUnassignedPool: true`).
   - Tasks can be reassigned, swapped, or moved up/down the hierarchy simply by changing their lineage label IDs.

## 💰 Bhakti Gamified Merit Matrix & Statutory Commission Slabs

To align corporate growth with meritocratic loyalty and Dubai real estate statutory transparency, the CRM enforces automated commission splits and gamified loyalty milestones:

### 1. Progressive Employee Commission Slabs

Employees progress through dynamic reward splits based on cumulative gross deal commissions closed within the rolling fiscal cycle:

| Gross Commission Tier (AED) | Base Split Rate | Merit Reward Tier         |
| --------------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------- |
| **AED 1 – 25,000**          | **50%**         | Standard Associate (L1)   |
| **AED 25,001 – 50,000**     | **55%**         | Senior Associate (L2)     |
| **AED 50,001 – 75,000**     | **60%**         | Enterprise Broker (L3)    |
| **AED 75,001 – 100,000**    | **65%**         | Principal Negotiator (L4) |
| **AED 100,001+**            | **70%**         | Sovereign Partner (L5)    |

- **Dynamic Metrics Managed**:
  - `merit_xp_count` (Int): Accumulated experience points earned through task completions, checklist audits, and zero-defect SLA execution.
  - `reward_tier_split` (Float): Real-time fractional split multiplier (0.50 to 0.70) applied directly during invoice clearance runs.

### 2. Client Performance Loyalty Milestones

Rewarding customer retention and high-frequency real estate portfolio growth:

- **Tenancy Loyalty:** Tenants completing their **3rd annual lease renewal** unlock a **10% cashback voucher** on agency service charges.
- **Investor Loyalty:** High-net-worth investors executing their **2nd asset acquisition** unlock a **10% commission cashback voucher** redeemable against future property conveyancing, conveyancing escrow management, or property staging services.
- **Voucher Attributes:** Tracked within `ClientProfile.loyaltyVouchers` with cryptographic validation codes, expiry timestamps, and FTA VAT adjustment records.

## 🚀 Architectural Technical Standards (Mandatory)

### 1. Four-Way File Separation — **ALL components**

Every UI component **must** use co-located four-way separation:

```text
ComponentName/
  ComponentName.tsx          # View only (presentation)
  ComponentName.logic.ts     # Behaviour / hooks / state wiring
  ComponentName.style.ts     # Styles / tokens only
  ComponentName.data.ts      # Static copy, maps, constants
```

Enforcement: see `docs/governance/FOUR_WAY_RULE.md` and CI checklist. New or changed components that violate this rule are not mergeable.

### 2. Deterministic state

Redux Toolkit with domain slices and memoized selectors (`createSelector`).

### 3. Quality gates

- `npm run typecheck` → **0** TypeScript errors
- Unit/integration tests via Vitest (lean, high-value)
- Critical path E2E via Playwright
- See `docs/governance/TESTING_POLICY.md`

### 4. Brand & z-index tokens

Only design tokens; no raw hex or ad-hoc z-index in feature code.
