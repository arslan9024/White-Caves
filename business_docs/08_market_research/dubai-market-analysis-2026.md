## 1. Market Overview (2025-2026)

### Key Metrics
- **Total transactions (2025):** 180,000+ (record year)
- **Transaction value (2025):** AED 522+ billion
- **Average price growth (2025):** +15% YoY
- **Rental yields:** 6-9% gross (among highest globally)
- **Off-plan share:** ~45% of all transactions

### Market Drivers
1. **Golden Visa program** — 10-year residency for property investors (AED 2M+)
2. **Dubai 2040 Urban Master Plan** — Population target: 5.8M (from 3.6M)
3. **Tax-free environment** — No income tax, no capital gains tax
4. **World-class infrastructure** — Expo 2020 legacy, new metro lines, airports
5. **Remote work hub** — Virtual working visa, digital nomad friendly
6. **Wealth migration** — HNWIs relocating from Europe, Asia, CIS countries

### Property Segments
| Segment | Price Range (AED) | Growth (YoY) | Key Areas |
|---------|-------------------|---------------|-----------|
| Ultra-luxury | 30M+ | +22% | Palm Jumeirah, Emirates Hills, Bluewaters |
| Luxury | 10M-30M | +18% | Dubai Marina, Downtown, DIFC |
| Premium | 3M-10M | +15% | JLT, Business Bay, MBR City |
| Mid-market | 1M-3M | +12% | JVC, Dubai South, Town Square |
| Affordable | <1M | +8% | International City, DSO, Arjan |
| Off-plan | Varies | +25% volume | Dubai South, MBR City, Dubailand |
