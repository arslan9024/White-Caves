### 2. COMMISSION & PAYOUT TERMS
- **Commission Entitlement:** In consideration of the services provided, the Client shall be entitled to a commission of **{{COMMISSION_PERCENTAGE}}%**.
- **Commission Definition:** Commission is defined as the net commission amount earned and received by the Company for the real estate deals successfully closed by the Client.
- **Commission Payout Timeline:** The Client shall be eligible for commission payout immediately after the amount has been received and successfully credited to the bank account of the Company. Payment to the Client will be processed within **three (3) working days** of bank clearance.

---

### 3. SUBSCRIPTION AND PAYMENT
- **Subscription Fee:** No subscription fee is payable by the Client for Category E (75/25 split). For fixed packages, fees are billed per agreed tier schedule.
- **Penalty Clause:** Where no subscription fee applies, previous provisions on bounced or delayed subscription payments do not apply.

---

### 4. SERVICE TERM & RENEWAL
- **Term:** This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and remain in effect for an initial term of **one (1) month**.
- **Automatic Renewal:** This Agreement shall automatically renew for additional one-month terms unless terminated in accordance with Clause 5 prior to the expiration of each monthly period.
- **Subscription Upgrade:** The Client may upgrade their subscription to higher package categories at any time upon written notice.

---

### 5. TERMINATION OF AGREEMENT
This Agreement may be terminated:
- At any time by either Party providing a minimum of **two (2) months' written notice**.
- By mutual written consent of both Parties.
- By the Company with immediate effect for any material breach of this Agreement.
- In the event of non-timely payment by the Client (where applicable), the Company reserves the right to terminate immediately with a penalty equal to two (2) months' standard subscription fees.

---

### 6. CONFIDENTIALITY & OPERATIONAL POLICIES
- **Confidentiality:** Both Parties agree to maintain strict confidentiality of all client data, deal structures, and proprietary business information.
- **Operational Hours:** Operational hours for administrative support and finance are **09:00 AM to 17:00 PM GST**, Monday through Friday. Operations are closed on Saturday and Sunday.

---

### 7. GENERAL PROVISIONS
- The Client under this Agreement has sole authority to close deals under this agreement and cannot enter agreements on behalf of unauthorized third parties.
- Ongoing real estate transactions cannot be terminated until the Client has received all commissions related to that transaction.
- The Client is solely responsible for compliance in their personal dealings; the Company holds no liability for unauthorized Client actions.
- **Governing Law:** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Emirate of Dubai, United Arab Emirates.
