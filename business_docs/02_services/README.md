## Service Portfolio Overview

White Caves operates across five primary service verticals, each targeting distinct segments of the Dubai and UAE real estate market.

### 1. Property Management

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Description** | End-to-end property management for residential and commercial assets, including tenant acquisition, rent collection, maintenance coordination, and financial reporting. |
| **Target Market** | Property owners with 5–500+ units; institutional landlords; overseas investors requiring local management. |
| **Revenue Model** | **Percentage of collected rent** (5–8%) + one-time onboarding fee (AED 500–2,000 per unit). Optional premium tier for concierge-level services at 10%. |
| **Key Differentiators** | AI-powered maintenance triage via WhatsApp, automated rent reminders, real-time owner dashboards, RERA-compliant reporting. |
| **Annual Revenue Potential** | AED 2M–8M at scale (500+ managed units). |

### 2. Sales Brokerage

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Description** | Residential and commercial sales brokerage — from lead generation through to DLD transfer completion. Covers secondary market resales and primary/off-plan developer partnerships. |
| **Target Market** | End-user buyers (expat professionals, families), high-net-worth investors, first-time homebuyers, international buyers (GCC, Europe, Asia). |
| **Revenue Model** | **Commission-based**: 2% of sale price (industry standard). Developer partnerships at 3–5% for off-plan. Referral fees for mortgage and insurance introductions. |
| **Key Differentiators** | AI-assisted lead scoring, WhatsApp-first communication, virtual tours, CRM-driven pipeline management, multilingual support (English/Arabic). |
| **Annual Revenue Potential** | AED 5M–20M (based on AED 250M–1B in transaction volume). |

### 3. Leasing Services

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Description** | Residential and commercial leasing — tenant sourcing, viewings, contract negotiation, Ejari registration, and move-in coordination. |
| **Target Market** | Landlords seeking quality tenants; corporate relocation departments; tenants relocating to Dubai. |
| **Revenue Model** | **Flat fee per lease**: 5% of annual rent (paid by tenant or landlord). Renewal commission at 2.5%. Furnished property premium (+AED 2,000). |
| **Key Differentiators** | Automated tenant screening (KYC/AML), digital contract signing, Ejari integration, AI-matched tenant-property recommendations. |
| **Annual Revenue Potential** | AED 1.5M–6M (500–2,000 leases annually). |

### 4. Facility Management

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Description** | Maintenance, cleaning, security, landscaping, and technical services for residential communities, commercial buildings, and mixed-use developments. |
| **Target Market** | Owners' associations (OAs), developers with post-handover obligations, commercial building operators, hospitality properties. |
| **Revenue Model** | **Monthly retainer** per unit/building (AED 200–800/unit/month) + variable cost pass-through for materials. Annual contracts with quarterly reviews. |
| **Key Differentiators** | WhatsApp-based maintenance requests, IoT sensor integration for predictive maintenance, SLA-driven vendor management, real-time work order tracking. |
| **Annual Revenue Potential** | AED 3M–12M (managing 1,000–5,000 units). |

### 5. Valuation Services

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Description** | RERA-certified property valuations for sales, mortgage applications, insurance, dispute resolution, and portfolio assessments. |
| **Target Market** | Banks and financial institutions, property owners, legal firms, government entities, investors requiring portfolio-level valuations. |
| **Revenue Model** | **Per-valuation fee**: Residential AED 2,500–5,000, Commercial AED 5,000–15,000, Portfolio (10+ units) at negotiated rates. Subscription model for repeat clients (banks). |
| **Key Differentiators** | AI-powered comparable analysis, market data enrichment, automated report generation, fast turnaround (48h standard, 24h express). |
| **Annual Revenue Potential** | AED 1M–4M (400–1,600 valuations annually). |

| ID | Criterion | Testable Condition |
|----|-----------|-------------------|
| PM-AC-001 | Onboarding within 5 business days | Signed management agreement → property on system within 5 days |
| PM-AC-002 | Rent collected and disbursed on schedule | 100% of rents collected by due date; disbursed to landlord within 3 business days |
| PM-AC-003 | Maintenance response within SLA | P1: 4h acknowledge; P2: 24h; P3: 72h; verified by ticket timestamp |
| PM-AC-004 | RERA-compliant tenancy contract | Ejari filed within 30 days of contract signing; verified in DLD system |
| PM-AC-005 | Monthly owner report by 5th of month | Report delivered by 5th; includes income, expenses, occupancy rate |
| PM-AC-006 | Tenant satisfaction NPS ≥ 40 | Monthly tenant survey; NPS tracked in analytics dashboard |

## RERA-Required Service Standards

### Mandatory RERA/DLD Compliance per Service Line

| Service | RERA/DLD Requirement | Regulatory Basis | White Caves Process |
|---------|---------------------|------------------|--------------------|
| **Property Management** | Written management agreement; RERA-licensed manager; RERA Standard Management Agreement template mandatory | RERA Circular 2019 | Management agreement generated via system; PM license verified before onboarding |
| **Sales Brokerage** | Form A (seller mandate) before listing; Form B (buyer mandate) before offer; DLD transfer within 30 days of acceptance; 4% DLD transfer fee collected | Dubai Law 85/2006; DLD Fee Schedule | System enforces form gates; DLD transfer tracker; commission splits comply with RERA dual-agency rules |
| **Leasing** | Ejari registration ≤ 30 days; RERA rental index compliance; no undisclosed fees beyond RERA standard commission (5%) | Dubai Law 26/2007; RERA Rental Index | Ejari auto-filed via DLD API; rental increase algorithm per RERA published index |
| **Off-Plan Sales** | Oqood DLD registration before handover; developer RERA-registered; escrow Law No. 8 of 2007 compliance | Dubai Law 8/2007; RERA Res. 6/2010 | Oqood tracker; escrow account field mandatory; developer RERA status verified |
| **Valuation** | RERA-certified valuer; RICS or UAE-approved methodology; report must follow RERA valuation standards | RERA Valuation Guidelines; RICS Red Book | Valuer credentials stored in system; report template RERA-compliant |
| **KYC/AML across all services** | CDD mandatory before transaction > AED 55,000; EDD for PEPs and high-risk; SAR via goAML | UAE AML Law 20/2019; Min. Resolution 45/2022 | Automated KYC workflow; PEP API screening; SAR draft auto-generated on flag |
| **Advertising** | RERA permit number on all listings; agent BRN on all marketing materials; accurate pricing (no bait-and-switch) | RERA Advertising Circular 2023 | System validates permit number + BRN before listing goes live |
