## Revenue Model Summary

### Primary Revenue Streams

| Stream | Rate | Trigger |
|--------|------|---------|
| Sale commission (primary) | 2% of sale price | SPA signed + DLD transfer |
| Sale commission (secondary) | 1–2% of sale price | Depends on off-plan vs resale |
| Rental commission (new lease) | 5% of annual rent | Lease signed + Ejari registered |
| Lease renewal commission | 2% of annual rent | Lease renewed |
| Property management | 5–8% of annual rent / month | Ongoing management agreement |
| Maintenance coordination | Fixed fee per job or 10% of invoice | Per maintenance order |

### Projected Revenue (Year 1 — Platform Supported)

| Revenue Source | Volume/Month | AED/Month | AED/Year |
|----------------|-------------|----------|---------|
| Sales transactions (avg AED 2M × 2%) | 8 deals | 320,000 | 3,840,000 |
| Rental new leases (avg AED 120K/yr × 5%) | 15 leases | 90,000 | 1,080,000 |
| Property management (50 units × 6%) | 50 units × AED 10K/mo | 30,000 | 360,000 |
| Lease renewals | 10/month | 20,000 | 240,000 |
| Referral fees | Variable | 10,000 | 120,000 |
| **Total** | | **~470,000** | **~5,640,000** |
