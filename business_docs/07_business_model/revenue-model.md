## 1. Revenue Stream Architecture

### Tier 1 — Transaction Revenue (Primary)

#### 1.1 Sales Commissions

| Transaction Type | Commission Rate | Notes |
|----------------|----------------|-------|
| Off-plan new sale (developer) | 5–7% of property price | Developer pays 100% |
| Off-plan secondary market | 2% of transaction | Split buyer/seller |
| Ready property (primary) | 2% | Buyer side |
| Ready property (secondary) | 1–2% | Both sides if dual agent |
| Commercial sale | 2–3% | Negotiated |
| Land sale | 1.5–2% | Per agreement |

**DLD Transfer Fee Responsibility (Client):**
- 4% of property price (buyer pays)
- AED 4,000 admin fee

#### 1.2 Rental Commissions

| Type | Rate | Paid By | Frequency |
|------|------|---------|-----------|
| New residential lease | 5% of annual rent | Landlord | One-time on new lease |
| New commercial lease | 8–10% of annual rent | Landlord | One-time on new lease |
| Lease renewal (managed) | 2% of annual rent | Landlord | Per renewal |
| Short-term rental setup | 10% of first month | Landlord | Per booking channel setup |

### Tier 2 — Recurring Revenue

#### 2.1 Property Management

| Service Level | Monthly Fee | Coverage |
|--------------|-------------|---------|
| Basic Management | 5% of monthly rent | Rent collection, tenant comms |
| Full Management | 8% of monthly rent | + Maintenance coordination, renewals |
| Premium Management | 10% of monthly rent | + Legal, compliance, reporting |

#### 2.2 Maintenance Coordination Fee

| Model | Rate |
|-------|------|
| Per job coordination | 10% of contractor invoice |
| Monthly retainer (>10 units) | AED 2,000/month flat |

### Tier 3 — Value-Added Services

| Service | Fee | Frequency |
|---------|-----|-----------|
| Mortgage referral (bank partner) | 0.5–1% of loan value | Per successful mortgage |
| Legal service referral | Fixed fee AED 500–1,000 | Per referral |
| Property valuation report | AED 2,500 | Per report |
| Photography + staging coordination | AED 1,500–5,000 | Per listing |
| Market analysis report | AED 5,000 | Per custom report |

## 4. Unit Economics

### Per Sales Transaction

```
Average off-plan transaction: AED 1,500,000 property value
Developer commission (7%):          AED 105,000
Agent payout (split 50/50 with company): AED 52,500
Company net per transaction:             AED 52,500
After marketing/overhead (~20%):         AED 42,000
```

### Per Managed Property (Monthly)

```
Average managed unit rent: AED 8,000/month
Management fee (6.5%):           AED 520/month
Annual fee per unit:              AED 6,240
Cost to service (30% overhead):   AED 1,872/unit/year
Net per unit/year:                AED 4,368
At 150 units:                     AED 655,200/year
```
