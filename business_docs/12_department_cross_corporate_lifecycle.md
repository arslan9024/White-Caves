## 🏢 Itemized Functional Breakdown of the 12 Specialized Departments

1. **Performance Marketing & Lead Acquisition (`marketing`)**:
   - Owns the inbound portal hooks.
   - Integrates API webhooks from Property Finder and Bayut.
   - Calculates Cost-Per-Lead (CPL) metrics via AI Olivia.

2. **Corporate Communications & Client Experience (`comms`)**:
   - Captures the lead stream.
   - Nadia WhatsApp CRM routing pipelines index the data stream.
   - Triggers an instant, red-pulsing 15-minute response SLA counter next to record rows.

3. **Portfolio Management & Residential Leasing (`leasing`)**:
   - Evaluates rental demand profiles.
   - Tracks active Ejari registrations (`0120250814005322`).
   - Processes local tenant/landlord documentation matrices.

4. **Residential Brokerage - Secondary Market (`sales`)**:
   - Commands client engagement.
   - Brokers execute physical property walk-through viewings and manage transaction negotiations under Supervisor oversight.

5. **Strategic Off-Plan & Project Development (`offplan`)**:
   - Synchronizes bulk developer pipelines.
   - Tracks project launch schedules and off-plan allocation tiers directly with primary Dubai builders (Emaar, DAMAC, Nakheel, Sobha).

6. **Asset Management & Facilities - DH2 Hub (`asset_mgmt`)**:
   - Drives on-ground logistics.
   - Oversees reactive maintenance dispatches and engineering checklists for 9,378+ managed properties in DAMAC Hills 2.

7. **Conveyancing & Transaction Management (`conveyancing`)**:
   - Processes deal closing.
   - Tracks Developer No Objection Certificate (NOC) sequences.
   - Schedules official Dubai Land Department (DLD) Trustee Office transfers.

8. **Legal, Compliance & RERA Affairs (`compliance`)**:
   - Enforces absolute regulatory safety.
   - Generates official Form A (Seller Contract), Form B (Buyer Contract), and Form I (Agent Split) sheets.
   - Validates Trakheesi permits and AML threat screenings under AI Laila.

9. **Revenue, Finance & Treasury (`finance`)**:
   - Executes transactional splits.
   - Math engine calculates commission tiers, tracks chronologically sorted Accounts Receivable (AR) aging columns, and processes standard 5% UAE VAT parameters.

10. **Market Intelligence & IoT Data Science (`intelligence`)**:
    - Feeds predictive modeling.
    - Sentinel telemetry modules track neighborhood appraisal metrics and pricing trend lines across Dubai clusters.

11. **Technology, AI & Systems (`tech`)**:
    - Maintains repository system performance.
    - Optimizes Nodemon hot-reload paths, local caching layers (`plans/AEGIS_CACHE.json`), and absolute path configurations.

12. **Executive Office & Corporate Governance (`executive`)**:
    - The "Vision Center" for Arsalan Malik to evaluate cross-department activity tickers, toggle the "Ghost Session" impersonation dropdown, and issue strategic directives.
