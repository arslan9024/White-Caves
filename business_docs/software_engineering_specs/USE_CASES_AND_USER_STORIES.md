## 1. Internal Corporate Machinery Stories

### US-01: Managing Director Sovereign Control (Level 5)
- **As the** Managing Director (Arslan Malik),
- **I want to** access the unrestricted dashboard cockpit unmasking all 12 corporate departments, 35 AI assistants, and executive ledgers,
- **So that I can** monitor real-time company revenue, approve high-ticket transactions, and govern company licenses.

### US-02: Licensed Broker Lead Conversion (Level 2)
- **As a** Licensed Real Estate Broker,
- **I want to** receive instant notifications for hot leads from Property Finder & Bayut with a 15-minute SLA countdown,
- **So that I can** contact clients immediately and auto-fill Form B viewing registers in 1 click.

## 2. Paired Client Portal Stories

### US-03: Tenant Lease Execution & E-Signature (Client Level 1)
- **As a** Prospective Tenant,
- **I want to** receive a secure digital link to review the Unified Tenancy Contract with PDC repayment schedule and sign on my mobile device,

### US-04: Landlord Net Rental Yield Tracking (Asset Owner Level 2)
- **As a** Property Landlord,
- **I want to** view my property occupancy status, rent collection dates, and download audited annual statements in AED or USD,
- **So that I can** track my net return on investment effortlessly.
