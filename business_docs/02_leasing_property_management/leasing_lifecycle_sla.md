## 2. Leasing Lifecycle Operations & SLAs

### 2.1 Deposit & Payment Processing
- **Security Deposit Standard:** 5% of annual rent for unfurnished units; 10% of annual rent for furnished units.
- **PDC Post-Dated Cheques:** Stored in secure digital vault; clearance dates tracked automatically 14 days prior to bank presentation.

### 2.2 Lease Renewal & Rent Index Adjustments
- **90-Day Renewal Window:** 90 days prior to Ejari expiration, system automatically queries the official RERA Rent Index Calculator.
- If rent increase is allowed, Form 7 notice is served electronically to tenant.
- If tenant agrees, renewed Ejari certificate is issued upon receipt of new PDC cheques.
