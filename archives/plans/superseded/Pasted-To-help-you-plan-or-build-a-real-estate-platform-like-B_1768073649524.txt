### 💡 Key Implementation Insights
*   **For the Frontend (User Platform)**: The components from the clone script provide a solid, feature-complete blueprint. Pay close attention to search performance and listing quality (e.g., mandatory floor plans).
*   **For the CRM**: This is typically a backend system for agents. You can build a custom module (like "In-App Lead Management") or integrate a third-party CRM like Salesforce or HubSpot. Key value lies in **automating follow-ups** and **centralizing communications**.
*   **Agent Tools Are Crucial**: A successful portal depends on attracting agents. Provide robust tools for **listing management**, **performance analytics**, and **client communication**.

I hope this detailed breakdown provides a strong foundation for your project. Are you more focused on the user experience for buyers and renters, or on developing the backend tools for agents and admins? Knowing your priority can help refine the next steps.
