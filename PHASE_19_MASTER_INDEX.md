## 📞 Communication & Support

### Daily Standups
- **Time:** 9:00 AM PT
- **Format:** 15 minutes (video + Slack async)
- **Frequency:** Monday-Friday
- **Attendees:** All team leads + support staff

### Weekly Sync Meetings
- **Performance Sync:** Thursdays 3:30 PM PT (1 hour)
- **Security Sync:** Tuesdays 2:00 PM PT (1 hour)
- **Reliability Sync:** Mondays 10:00 AM PT (1 hour)
- **Cost Review:** Wednesdays 11:00 AM PT (30 min)
- **Observability Review:** Fridays 2:00 PM PT (30 min)

### Executive Reviews
- **Weekly:** VP Engineering (Fridays 5:00 PM PT, 30 min)
- **Bi-weekly:** CTO + Directors (Thursdays 4:00 PM PT, 1 hour)
- **Monthly:** CEO + CFO (Last Friday of month, 1 hour)

### Escalation Path
1. **Blocker:** Report immediately to Workstream Lead
2. **Critical Risk:** Escalate to VP Engineering
3. **Strategic Decision:** Escalate to CTO/VP Engineering
4. **Business Impact:** Escalate to CEO (via CTO)
