## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Tuesday People & Minds initiative integrates Nancy (HR Manager), Linda (WhatsApp Communications), and Zoe (Executive Intelligence) into a unified recruitment ecosystem designed to:

 **Reduce cost-per-hire by 54%** (AED 15,500  AED 7,200)  
 **Accelerate hiring velocity by 45%** (40  20 days)  
 **Automate 95% of hiring processes**  
 **Save AED 1.785M in Year 1** (payback Month 16)  
 **Improve retention by 12%** (85%  95% 90-day retention)  

**Total Investment:** AED 2.26M | **Year 1 Savings:** AED 1.785M | **Year 2 Recurring:** AED 2.4-2.8M

## RISK MANAGEMENT

### Top 5 Risks & Mitigations

**Risk 1: Resume Screening AI Inaccuracy**
- Impact: Missed qualified candidates
- Mitigation: Human review queue for 40-70 score range, 95%+ accuracy target, weekly validation
- Owner: Data Scientist

**Risk 2: Poor HR Team Adoption**
- Impact: System unused, no benefits
- Mitigation: Comprehensive training, change management, success stories, incentives
- Owner: HR Director

**Risk 3: System Integration Failures**
- Impact: Data loss, candidate experience degradation
- Mitigation: Data validation, daily reconciliation, backups, testing strategy
- Owner: CTO

**Risk 4: Budget Overruns**
- Impact: ROI becomes negative
- Mitigation: Weekly budget reviews, change control, contingency reserve (AED 240K)
- Owner: Finance Director

**Risk 5: Candidate Experience Issues**
- Impact: Reduced application quality
- Mitigation: Satisfaction monitoring, human fallback, responsive support
- Owner: Communications Director
