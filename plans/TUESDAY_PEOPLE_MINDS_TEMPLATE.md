# TUESDAY: People & Minds - Team Development & Culture
**Updated:** January 17, 2026

## 📋 Executive Summary
Tuesday focuses on people development, culture building, and relationship management. This is the day dedicated to strengthening internal teams and external partnerships.

## 🎯 Core Objectives

- **Team Development**: Build skills and career growth
- **Culture Building**: Foster positive work environment
- **Client Relations**: Strengthen customer relationships
- **Network Expansion**: Build strategic partnerships
