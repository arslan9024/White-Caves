## 🔍 RISK ASSESSMENT

### White Caves' ML/FT Risk Profile

**Risk Level:** MEDIUM-HIGH (for real estate DNFBP)

**High-Risk Transaction Characteristics:**
- Property value **> AED 5,000,000**
- **Cash payments** or third-party involvement
- **International clients** or offshore companies
- **PEPs** or family members of PEPs
- Rapid series of purchases/sales (property portfolio building)
- Unusual payment methods or delays
- Complex ownership structures (trusts, multiple layers)
- Clients from high-risk jurisdictions

**Medium-Risk Transaction Characteristics:**
- Property value **AED 500,000 - AED 5,000,000**
- Individual UAE residents (expats)
- Standard mortgage or finance arrangements
- Reasonable economic justification
- 1-2 property transactions per year

**Low-Risk Transaction Characteristics:**
- Property value **< AED 500,000**
- UAE nationals with verified income
- Residential properties only
- Single or occasional transactions
- Clear source of funds (salary, savings)

## 🔐 CUSTOMER DUE DILIGENCE (CDD) FRAMEWORK

### CDD Process Flow

```
New Customer Inquiry
        ↓
Collect Initial Information
        ↓
Risk Assessment (Low/Medium/High)
        ↓
Generate CDD Form (appropriate level)
        ↓
Customer Verification Documents
        ├─ Identity (Passport/Emirates ID/Visa)
        ├─ Address (Utility bill or lease)
        ├─ Employment (Letter/Contract)
        └─ Financial (Bank statement/Tax return)
        ↓
Beneficial Ownership (if company/trust)
        ↓
PEP Screening (if high-risk)
        ↓
Source of Funds Verification
        ↓
Risk Approval by Compliance Officer
        ├─ APPROVED → Proceed with transaction
        ├─ CONDITIONAL → Request more information
        └─ REJECTED → Escalate/Report to FIU
        ↓
Record Filing & Ongoing Monitoring
```

### CDD Requirements by Transaction Level

#### LEVEL 1: LOW-RISK CUSTOMERS
**Triggers:** Individual, UAE national, residential, <AED 500K, single transaction

**Required Documents:**
- ✓ Emirates ID (both sides)
- ✓ Proof of address (utility bill, max 3 months old)
- ✓ Employment verification or salary slip
- ✓ Source of funds statement (savings, salary, sale proceeds)

**CDD Form:** Basic form (5 fields, 10 minutes)  
**Approval:** Arslan Malik (verbal or email)  
**Processing Time:** 1-2 business days

#### LEVEL 2: MEDIUM-RISK CUSTOMERS
**Triggers:** Expat, commercial property, AED 500K-5M, 2-3 properties, mortgage involved

**Required Documents:**
- ✓ Passport + Visa/Residency
- ✓ Emirates ID
- ✓ Proof of address (utility bill or lease contract)
- ✓ Employment letter + recent salary slips (3 months)
- ✓ Bank statements (6 months) showing funds
- ✓ If self-employed: Business registration + tax returns (2 years)

**CDD Form:** Enhanced form (15 fields, 20 minutes)  
**Beneficial Ownership:** If company buyer, list all owners >10%  
**Approval:** Arslan Malik + review by legal (Anna Petrova)  
**Processing Time:** 3-5 business days

#### LEVEL 3: HIGH-RISK CUSTOMERS
**Triggers:** >AED 5M, PEP, international entity, cash deal, third-party involvement

**Required Documents:**
- ✓ Full passport + all visas
- ✓ Emirates ID
- ✓ Official company documents (MOA, board resolution, business license)
- ✓ Bank statements (12 months)
- ✓ Tax returns (3 years)
- ✓ Beneficial ownership certification (down to natural persons)
- ✓ PEP screening results
- ✓ Source of funds detailed explanation
- ✓ Transaction purpose documentation

**CDD Form:** Full Enhanced Due Diligence (30+ fields, 1-2 hours)  
**PEP Screening:** OFAC, UN, EU, local lists checked  
**Beneficial Ownership:** Trace all ownership layers, identify ultimate beneficial owner

**Approval:** Arslan Malik + Legal (Anna) + Senior Management approval  
**Processing Time:** 7-10 business days

## 🚨 SUSPICIOUS TRANSACTION INDICATORS (Red Flags)

### Real Estate Specific Red Flags

**Customer Behavior Indicators:**
1. Customer reluctant to provide standard identification
2. Customer uses multiple agents for single transaction
3. Customer changes transaction terms frequently
4. Customer pressures agent to rush deal completion
5. Customer avoids documentation or verification
6. Customer requests unusual payment structures
7. Customer uses attorney/representative without clear reason

**Transaction Pattern Indicators:**
1. **Large cash payments** (AED 55,000+) without clear justification
2. **Multiple properties** purchased in short timeframe
3. **Rapid property flips** (buy/sell within 1-3 months)
4. **Series of transactions** with no clear investment pattern
5. **Mixed property types** without business rationale
6. **Overpaying or underpaying** compared to market value (>20% variance)
7. **No clear occupancy plan** (vacant, no business use stated)

**Financial Indicators:**
1. **Third-party payments** without clear relationship to buyer
2. **International transfers** from high-risk countries
3. **Structured payments** designed to avoid AED 55K threshold
4. **Loan mismatches** (mortgage approved for different amount)
5. **Frequent fund transfers** before closing
6. **Wire transfers** from unknown sources
7. **Cash "gifts"** from undisclosed parties

**Documentation Issues:**
1. **Forged or altered documents** (PassID signatures, ID photos inconsistent)
2. **Documents in different names** (signatory vs. identified person)
3. **Mismatched information** (documents show different addresses)
4. **Missing pages** or incomplete documents
5. **Outdated documents** (expired passports, old utility bills)
6. **Document inconsistencies** (nationality changes, name variations)

**Geographic/Jurisdiction Indicators:**
1. Client from **high-risk jurisdictions** (Iran, North Korea, Syria, etc.)
2. Client with **sanctioned country connections**
3. Buyer from jurisdiction with weak AML/CFT controls
4. Offshore company as buyer without clear business purpose

**Customer Profile Mismatches:**
1. **Student purchasing luxury villa** (income/age mismatch)
2. **Retired person** with large income sources
3. **Unemployed person** with substantial funds
4. **Age inappropriate** (minor, very elderly without proper authority)
5. **Professional inconsistencies** (title/qualifications don't match income)

## 📋 SUSPICIOUS TRANSACTION REPORT (STR) PROCEDURE

### STR Filing Process

**Step 1: Detection & Escalation (Immediate)**
- Agent identifies suspicious activity
- Agent reports to Arslan Malik (Compliance Officer) immediately
- NO DELAY in reporting (do not alert customer)
- Maintain confidentiality (don't discuss with others)

**Step 2: Investigation (5 Business Days)**
- Arslan reviews transaction details
- Gathers additional documentation
- Interviews relevant staff
- Reviews customer's other transactions
- Documents all findings in STR log

**Step 3: STR Decision (Day 5)**
- Arslan determines: Is STR required?
- Reviews against red flag indicators
- Documents decision rationale
- Approves/rejects STR filing

**Step 4: goAML Submission (If Required)**
- Arslan logs into goAML portal (https://services.uaefiu.gov.ae/goaml/)
- Fills STR form with:
  - Customer information
  - Transaction details
  - Property details
  - Suspicious indicators identified
  - Investigation findings
  - Supporting documents
- Submits to FIU
- Records submission details (date, time, STR number)

**Step 5: Record & Follow-up (Ongoing)**
- File STR record in compliance vault
- Maintain documentation for 5 years
- Monitor customer's future transactions
- Do NOT alert customer (confidentiality requirement)
- Await FIU feedback/investigation

**STR Submission Success Checklist:**
- ✓ Form completed accurately
- ✓ All required fields filled
- ✓ Supporting documents attached
- ✓ Submitted within 5 business days
- ✓ FIU confirmation number recorded
- ✓ Internal audit trail documented
