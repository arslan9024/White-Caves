## Mission

Generate high-quality leads, build the White Caves brand as a luxury Dubai real estate authority, and drive engagement across digital and physical channels. The Marketing department maximises property visibility, measures ROI on all campaigns, and continuously improves the buyer journey.

## Key Responsibilities

1. **Campaign Management** — Plan, launch, and optimise property marketing campaigns via Olivia.
2. **Social Media Management** — Manage Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, and YouTube for White Caves.
3. **Property Listing Optimisation** — Optimise Bayut, Property Finder, Dubizzle, and company website listings.
4. **SEO & Content** — Maintain a content calendar; publish market insights, property guides, and news.
5. **PPC Advertising** — Manage Google Ads and Meta Ads campaigns for lead generation.
6. **Brand Governance** — Ensure all communications align with the White Caves luxury brand guide.
7. **Agent Performance Coaching** — Coach sales agents on personal branding and client communication via Apex.
8. **Client Satisfaction Tracking** — Collect NPS scores and feedback post-transaction via Halo.
9. **Email & WhatsApp Marketing** — Run drip campaigns and broadcast messages to opted-in lists.
10. **Event Marketing** — Organise property launch events, open houses, and investor briefings.
11. **Competitor Monitoring** — Track competitor pricing, listings, and campaigns.
12. **Visual Content Production** — Professional photography, virtual tours, and 3D renders coordination.

### End-to-End Marketing Flow

```
New Property Onboarded (Operations → Mary)
  ↓
Olivia creates marketing brief:
  - Property type, location, price, USPs
  - Target audience (buyer/investor/tenant)
  ↓
Content created:
  - Photography/video (physical)
  - AI staging images (Iris)
  ↓
Listings published:
  - Company website (SEO optimised)
  - Bayut, Property Finder, Dubizzle
  - Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok
  ↓
PPC campaigns activated:
  - Google Ads (search + display)
  - Meta Ads (Instagram/Facebook)
  - WhatsApp broadcast (Nadia)
  ↓
Leads captured → Clara (Sales) notified
  ↓
Campaign tracked:
  - Impressions, clicks, enquiries, cost-per-lead
  - Oracle/Cipher analytics integration
  ↓
Performance report generated (Lumen)
  ↓
ROI assessed; budget reallocated to best performers

Post-Transaction Flow:
  → Deal closed (Sales)
  → Halo sends NPS survey (WhatsApp + email)
  → Score collected; feedback logged
  → Apex reviews agent's client feedback
  → Coaching suggestions generated for low-scoring agents
```
