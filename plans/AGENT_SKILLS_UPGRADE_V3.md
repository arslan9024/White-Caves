### Research Output Standards

Every research output must include:

1. **Executive Summary** (≤ 3 bullets, actionable)
2. **Key Findings** (numbered, evidence-backed)
3. **Risk Flags** (if any blockers or compliance risks found)
4. **Recommended Next Actions** (concrete, addressed to a named agent or team)
5. **Sources** (URLs, spec links, or DLD/RERA doc references)
6. **FEEDS→** handoff tag to the relevant delivery team
