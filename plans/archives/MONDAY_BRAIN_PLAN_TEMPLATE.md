## 💡 Innovation Pipeline

### Idea Evaluation Criteria

1. **Market Demand** (0-10): Does the market need this?
2. **Feasibility** (0-10): Can we build/implement this?
3. **ROI Potential** (0-10): What's the financial upside?
4. **Timeline** (0-10): Can we execute in reasonable time?
5. **Alignment** (0-10): Does this fit our strategy?

**Scoring Threshold**: Ideas scoring 35+ advance to research phase

### Innovation Pipeline Stages

1. **Ideation**: Initial concept phase
2. **Research**: Feasibility and market analysis
3. **Development**: Prototype or MVP creation
4. **Testing**: Market validation
5. **Implementation**: Full rollout
