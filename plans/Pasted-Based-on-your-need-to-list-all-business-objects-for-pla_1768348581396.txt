### 🧱 Core Property Data Objects
The work of developers and brokers revolves around managing detailed property data, which your platform will need to model. From examples of developer projects, these objects typically include:

*   **Project**: The main development (e.g., "EVERGR1N HOUSE" in Jumeirah Garden City).
*   **Building/Tower**: A structure within a project (e.g., "LUM1NAR TOWER 1").
*   **Unit/Apartment**: An individual property for sale or rent, defined by:
    *   **Type**: Studio, 1, 2, 3+ Bedrooms.
    *   **Floor Plan**: Specific layout (e.g., "TYPE A") with precise square footage.
    *   **Price**: Starting price or price-per-unit.
*   **Amenity**: A feature of the project or building (e.g., gym, pool, kids' play area, clubhouse).

### 🗺️ Planning Your Project Structure
To plan your project, map these business entities to your previously discussed OOP classes. Here’s a suggested high-level mapping to structure your platform's domains:

```yaml
1. Portfolio & Asset Management Domain:
   - Objects: `Property`, `Project`, `Building`, `Unit`, `Amenity`
   - Relations: Tracks ownership, specifications, and status.

2. Transaction & Brokerage Domain:
   - Objects: `Listing`, `Offer`, `Client` (Buyer/Seller/Tenant), `Agent`, `Commission`
   - Relations: Manages the sales/rental lifecycle and client relationships.

3. Operations & Service Domain:
   - Objects: `ServiceRequest`, `MaintenanceTicket`, `TenancyContract`, `Payment`
   - Relations: Handles post-sale property management and owner services.

4. Intelligence & Advisory Domain:
   - Objects: `MarketReport`, `Valuation`, `InvestmentPortfolio`, `AIRecommendation`
   - Relations: Powers data analytics and AI-driven insights for clients.
```

This structure allows you to cluster related functionality, making your platform modular and scalable to handle everything from unit sales to portfolio management.
