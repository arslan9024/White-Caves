## New Hire Buddy Program

### How Buddies Are Assigned

| Criteria | Standard |
|---------|---------|
| Role match | Buddy has the same or senior role as new hire |
| Experience | Buddy has minimum 12 months at White Caves |
| Performance | Buddy is in "Meets Expectations" or above band |
| Workload | Buddy not already mentoring another new hire |
| Assignment process | Manager selects buddy by Day -1 (before start) |

### Buddy Responsibilities

During the 90-day buddy period:

| Week | Commitment | Activity |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Week 1 | Daily check-in (15 min) | Answer questions, introduce to people |
| Week 2-4 | 3× per week (15 min) | Debrief on calls/viewings, share tips |
| Month 2 | Weekly (30 min) | Review progress, discuss challenges |
| Month 3 | Fortnightly (30 min) | Final push to independence |
| Day 90 | 1 hour | Buddy completion review + handover to manager |

### Buddy Recognition

- Buddy service recorded in buddy's performance file (positive contribution)
- Buddy performance bonus credit: completing a buddy mentorship = +3% toward that quarter's scorecard "Agent Development" KPI
- After 3 successful buddy completions: eligibility for "Mentor Certificate" + LinkedIn recommendation from MD
