## 30-Day Goals

By end of Day 30, the new agent should:

| Goal | Measurement |
|------|------------|
| Have their RERA profile fully set up + verified | RERA portal + CRM record |
| Have conducted their first 3 viewings | CRM activity log |
| Have added 5 qualified leads to CRM | CRM — 5 leads in QUALIFIED status |
| Understand DAMAC Hills 2 community (can pitch to a client) | Manager assessment |
| Know how to use all 8 CRM modules | Training quiz pass (80%+) |
| Have completed all compliance training modules | Training records |
| Have a monthly performance target agreed | Signed KPI agreement |

## Leaver Offboarding Checklist

When an agent or staff member leaves:

| Task | Owner | Timing |
|------|-------|--------|
| Notify all clients of agent change | Manager | Day of notice |
| Reassign all active leads | Sales Manager | Day of notice |
| Reassign all active listings (Form A transfer) | Compliance | Within 5 days |
| Remove CRM access | IT (Atlas) | Last day |
| Remove email access | IT (Atlas) | Last day |
| Remove WhatsApp Business access | IT | Last day |
| Collect laptop, access card | Office Manager | Last day |
| Final commission settlement | Finance (Theodora) | Within 30 days |
| RERA: update agent association (remove from company) | Compliance | Within 7 days |
| Return all client documents | Compliance | Last day |
| Exit interview completed | HR | Last day |
