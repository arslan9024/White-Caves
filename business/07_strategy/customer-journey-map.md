## Journey Map Structure

Each map covers:
- **Phases** — stages of the customer journey
- **Actions** — what the customer does
- **Touchpoints** — where they interact with White Caves
- **Emotions** — how they feel at each stage
- **Pain Points** — what frustrates them today
- **White Caves Differentiators** — how we make their experience better

## 5. Emotion Curve Summary

```
BUYER:
Curious → Research fatigue → Excited → Nervous → Relieved ✓

SELLER:
Hopeful → Impatient (viewings) → Relieved (offer) → Satisfied (sale) ✓

LANDLORD:
Skeptical → Engaged → Confident → Passive (if smooth) → Loyal ✓

TENANT:
Stressed → Worried → Settled → Routine → Satisfied (if easy) ✓

KEY INSIGHT: The biggest emotional dip across all personas is the
**transaction/paperwork phase** (DLD, Ejari, KYC documents).
White Caves' biggest differentiator opportunity is removing this friction
through portal automation, digital signing, and guided step-by-step flows.
```
