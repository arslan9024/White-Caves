## OKR Principles at White Caves

1. **Objectives** are ambitious, qualitative, inspiring goals
2. **Key Results** are measurable outcomes (not tasks or outputs)
3. Target attainment: 70% = success (100% means not ambitious enough)
4. Maximum 3–5 OKRs per department per quarter
5. Company OKRs cascade to department OKRs
6. Reviewed monthly; reset quarterly
