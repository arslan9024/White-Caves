### **4.4 Property Valuation & Pricing Strategy**

#### **Valuation Methodology:**
1. **Comparative Market Analysis (CMA)**
   - Recent sales in same building/community
   - Price per sqft benchmarks
   - View premium calculations
   - Floor premium calculations

2. **Income Approach** (for investment properties)
   - Rental yield analysis (4-8% in Dubai)
   - Cap rate calculations
   - Net operating income projections

3. **Replacement Cost Method**
   - Land value + construction cost
   - Developer premium for branded residences
   - Amenity premium calculation

#### **Pricing Strategy by Segment:**

| **Property Segment** | **Price Range (AED)** | **Commission Rate** | **Days on Market** | **Marketing Budget** |
