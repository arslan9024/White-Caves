### **DESIGN PATTERNS RECOMMENDATION**

1.  **Factory Pattern**: For creating different `Dashboard` views for your **6 user types**.
2.  **Strategy Pattern**: For interchangeable **AI algorithms** in `AgentAssignmentEngine`.
3.  **Observer Pattern**: For real-time notifications when `Property` status changes.
4.  **Facade Pattern**: For simplifying complex **UAE PASS** and **DLD API** integrations.
5.  **Singleton Pattern**: For `CompanyKnowledgeBase` and `AICoordinator`.
6.  **Decorator Pattern**: For adding features to core `Property` objects (e.g., `PropertyWithVirtualTour`).

This OOP structure provides **modularity, scalability, and clear separation of concerns**—critical for managing 32 AI assistants, 6 user types, and complex UAE real estate workflows.
