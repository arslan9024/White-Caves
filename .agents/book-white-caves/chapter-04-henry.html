1. Architectural Evolution & Modular Architecture

In accordance with the AEGIS sub-350 line rule, the monolithic scanner service was decomposed into 5 high-cohesion sub-modules under src/services/emiratesId/:

  • parser.logic.ts: TD1 ICAO MRZ and OCR entity parser with Luhn check-digit verification.
  • ocrEngine.logic.ts: Bounded multi-page rasterizer, bilingual Tesseract.js workers (eng+ara), adaptive rotation recovery (0°, 90°, 180°, 270°), and deadline watchdog.
  • pdfRenderer.logic.ts: Safe client-side canvas rasterization with memory budgets.
  • merger.logic.ts: Front/back dual-side card unification and DLD contract party conversion.
  • samples.data.ts: Synthetic benchmarks for safe offline testing without real PII exposure.
