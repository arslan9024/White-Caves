Chapter 6: Domain Actors & Entity-Action-Structure Matrix

Curated by @Aurora, Principal Systems CTO & Canonical Chronicler

Statutory Invariant (TURING-SEPARATION-010):
Clean decoupling of Actors, Actions, and Structure across all systems:
• Where they belong (DMS): 12 Fixed Corporate Departments (Floors 1–13).
• Who & What exists (ERMS): Pure Entities separated into Actors (Users, Clients, Developers, Banks) and Objects (Properties, Units, Accounts, Permits). Zero verbs or task steps live here.
• What they do (TMS): Actions and tasks (Verbs: VERIFY_KYC, GENERATE_EJARI, COLLECT_RENT). Every task connects an Actor within a Department.

1. The 18 Canonical Platform Actors

Group A: Internal Platform & Workforce Identities

Internal Identity
USER

The foundational authentication and session principal (email, password hash, TOTP RFC-6238, Firebase sync). Authenticates all system participants.

Security Control
ROLE (RBAC)

App permissions and floor clearance levels (Levels 1–6). Completely decoupled from HR job titles via RoleAssignment.

HR / Personnel
EMPLOYEE

Corporate employment record (employee code, department designation, labor card, and contract details).

Gamification & Performance
STAFF_PROFILE

Operational stats, Merit XP (+500 per statutory gate under AEG-037), and dynamic 60%–70% commission split engine (AEG-038).

Autonomous Mesh
AI_ASSISTANT

Autonomous and co-pilot agents within the 235-agent AEGIS mesh (Zoe COO, Ada, Elena, and the 120 departmental supervisors).

Recruitment
CANDIDATE

Recruitment prospects undergoing CV parsing, scoring, RERA broker license checks, and automated onboarding to Employee.

Group B: Transacting Parties & Client Personas

CRM Master Entity
CLIENT

The single source of truth for transacting persons/entities. Retains KYC, passports, nationality, and communication history.

Property Owner (Leasing)
LANDLORD

Legal title deed holder listing units for lease; signs leasing agreements and authorizes Ejari registrations.

Lease Occupant
TENANT

Active or prospective lessee; submits tenancy documents, PDC cheques, and signs Unified Ejari contracts.

Property Purchaser
BUYER

Purchaser in off-plan or secondary markets; submits Source of Wealth/POF, signs Form F (MOU), and funds escrow.

Property Vendor
SELLER

Secondary market vendor holding Title Deed (Mulkiya); executes Form A marketing agreements and developer NOC clearance.

External / Internal Broker
BROKER_AGENT

Licensed agent holding a RERA Broker Registration Number (BRN); governed by Form I broker-to-broker agreements.

Group C: Institutional & Dubai Real Estate Infrastructure

Master / Private Developer
DEVELOPER

Off-plan project developers (Emaar, DAMAC, Meraas, Sobha, Aldar). Releases inventory, executes SPAs, and grants resale NOCs.

DLD Authorized Transfer Office
REGISTRATION_TRUSTEE

Authorized DLD offices (e.g., Al Tabo) conducting secondary transfers, collecting 4% DLD fees, and issuing e-Title Deeds.

Financial Institution
MORTGAGE_LENDER

Commercial banks (ADCB, Emirates NBD, FAB). Issues Pre-Approvals, final offer letters (FOL), and mortgage liability release letters.

Corporate Holding Vehicle
CORPORATE_SPV

DIFC / ADGM / RAK ICC special purpose vehicles holding luxury real estate assets for liability ring-fencing and estate planning.

Community & HOA Authority
MOLLAK_ASSOCIATION

DLD-governed system for service charge clearance. No developer NOC or secondary title transfer can proceed without Mollak clearance.

Facility & Works
CONTRACTOR

Maintenance providers, snagging inspectors, and fit-out teams executing property handovers and maintenance SLAs.

2. Resolving Role Overlaps: The Dynamic Entity-Role Pattern

In high-end Dubai real estate, a client who purchases an off-plan penthouse as a BUYER becomes the LANDLORD when leasing it out, or later a SELLER upon exit.

Database Resolution: A single Client record exists with a multi-role classification tag. The role is contextualized through ClientProperty.relationship (owner, tenant, buyer, interested) and transactional foreign keys. This guarantees zero contact duplication and 100% unified financial history.
