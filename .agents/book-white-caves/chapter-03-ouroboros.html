Chapter 3: Protocol Perpetual Ouroboros

Curated by @Nova, Crucible Commander & @Grace, CTO

AEGIS Protocol: Perpetual Ouroboros is the 100% autonomous, self-healing continuous software development engine. It requires zero human interaction and guarantees ≥ 5% codebase optimization on every wave.

The 7 Autonomous Lifecycle Phases

Phase 1 The Deep Radar Scan (@Elena / @Nova)

Continuous diagnostic sweeps across all 10 architectural domains to detect gaps and compliance risks.

Phase 2 SRS & Software Planning (@Aurora / @Margaret)

Autonomous generation of software requirements and wave targets saved directly to .agents/plans/.

Phase 3 Department Matrix Delegation (Depts 01–12)

Dispatching targeted assignments to the 108 engineering specialists.

Phase 4 Self-Healing & Zero-Token Implementation (@Grace)

Autonomous code refactoring, deduplication, and dead-code pruning.

Phase 5 SQA Verification & Defensive Gates (@Rachel_QA / @Sofia)

Targeted Vitest runs and TypeScript strictness verification.

Phase 6 Gate Sign-off & Telemetry Sync (@Gwynne / @Zoe)

Clean Git commits and progress updates in .agents/project-progress.md.

Phase 7 Self-Learning & Instant Re-ignition (@Ada)

Recording lessons in .agents/docs/aegis-learning-report.md and triggering the next loop immediately.
