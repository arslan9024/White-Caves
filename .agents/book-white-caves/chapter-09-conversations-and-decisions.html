ADR-09.1: Sovereign Repository Decoupling (Public Web vs. Enterprise CRM)

Status: APPROVED & ACTIVE
Date: 2026-11-13
Decision Maker: Managing Director & @Ada / @Aurora
Impacted Repos: White-Caves-Web & White-Caves-Frontend

Context: As the White Caves project expanded to include 12 corporate brokerage departments, 235 AI agents, DLD/Ejari integrations, and client portals, bundling public marketing landing pages with the private enterprise CRM created bundle bloat, SEO compromises, and deployment friction.

Decision: Formal separation into two autonomous code repositories:

  • White-Caves-Web (https://github.com/arslan9024/White-Caves-Web.git): Public-facing luxury marketing engine deployed to whitecaves.ae / www.whitecaves.ae on Vercel. Optimized for Core Web Vitals, SSR/SSG, and luxury buyer lead acquisition.
  • White-Caves-Frontend (White-Caves-Frontend.git): Private Enterprise CRM/ERP operations platform deployed to app.whitecaves.ae. Optimized for high-throughput Redux state, broker transaction flows, and Zoe AI operations.
