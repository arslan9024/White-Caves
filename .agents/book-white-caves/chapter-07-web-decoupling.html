Sovereign Architecture Axiom: The Decoupled Twin

In accordance with TURING-LAW-000 and TURING-SEPARATION-010, the White Caves public digital experience was bifurcated into a high-performance, server-rendered Next.js marketing engine (whitecaves.com) and an ultra-secure, stateful Vite/Redux CRM operations portal (app.whitecaves.com).

2. Architectural Synthesis & Principles

  • Sovereign Zero-Waste (AEG-042): Every newly ported component remains under 350 lines, isolating markup from static datasets.
  • SEO & Core Web Vitals Dominance: Next.js 16 static pre-rendering delivers sub-100ms First Contentful Paint (FCP) and near-instant Largest Contentful Paint (LCP).
  • Continuous Chronicle Integration: As features advance through Wave 2, this chronicle updates automatically to reflect architectural truth.
