## 📊 Risk Assessment & Mitigation

### Risk 1: UAT Finds Critical Issues
**Probability**: Low (15%)
**Impact**: High (3-5 day delay)
**Mitigation**:
- Hotfix procedure documented
- Re-test plan in place
- Escalation path clear
- Alternative dates available

### Risk 2: Performance Issues Under Load
**Probability**: Low (10%)
**Impact**: High (optimization required)
**Mitigation**:
- Performance tests planned
- Caching strategy defined
- Database optimization ready
- Load testing infrastructure available

### Risk 3: Data Issues During Migration
**Probability**: Very Low (2%)
**Impact**: Critical (data recovery required)
**Mitigation**:
- Backup & restore tested
- Migration rollback procedure documented
- Data validation scripts prepared
- DBA on-call during deployment

### Risk 4: Deployment Window Too Short
**Probability**: Low (5%)
**Impact**: Medium (need extended window)
**Mitigation**:
- 4-hour window allocated
- Pre-deployment tasks completed
- Team well-trained
- Rollback can complete in 30 seconds

### Risk 5: Monitoring Not Detecting Issues
**Probability**: Low (10%)
**Impact**: High (users suffer)
**Mitigation**:
- Multiple monitoring tools (Datadog, CloudWatch, ELK)
- Automated rollback triggers set up
- Manual monitoring by team
- Alert thresholds tested
