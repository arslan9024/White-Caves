## Executive Summary

The Dubai real estate technology market is fragmented across portal platforms (PropertyFinder, Bayut, Dubizzle), international property management software (Yardi), regional PropTech startups (RealCube), and investment platforms (SmartCrowd). White Caves differentiates through its **WhatsApp-first CRM**, **AI-powered lead scoring**, and **luxury-focused design** targeting mid-to-premium brokerages operating in the Dubai market.

No single competitor combines CRM, property management, AI assistance, and portal syndication in a unified platform purpose-built for the UAE regulatory environment (RERA, DLD, Ejari).
