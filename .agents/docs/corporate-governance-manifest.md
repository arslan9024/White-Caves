## 3. Executive Commission Acceleration & Buffer Rules

### 3.1 Onboarding Broker Accelerator (180-Day Rule)
- All newly registered brokers receive an **accelerated 70/30 commission split** (70% Broker / 30% Agency) during their initial **180 days** from RERA card issuance.
- Post-180 days, commission splits transition dynamically based on rolling quarterly sales volume:
  - **Tier 1 (AED 0 – 5M Volume):** 50% Broker / 50% Agency
  - **Tier 2 (AED 5M – 15M Volume):** 60% Broker / 40% Agency
  - **Tier 3 (AED 15M+ Volume):** 70% Broker / 30% Agency

### 3.2 Performance Grace Period (3-Month Buffer)
- Squad Supervisors and Brokers falling below quarterly minimum targets are granted a mandatory **3-month grace period buffer**.
- During the buffer period, dedicated coaching by Department Managers is enforced before any downward tier migration or squad re-assignment occurs.

## 4. Administrative Escalation & Approval Thresholds

- **Deals ≤ AED 5,000,000**: Approved directly by Department Manager (Level 4).
- **Deals > AED 5,000,000**: Requires dual sign-off from Department Manager and Managing Director (Level 5).
- **Commission Release Approvals**: Multi-stage state check (`AGENT_SUBMITTED` ➔ `MANAGER_APPROVED` ➔ `FINANCE_LOCKED` ➔ `PAYMENT_RELEASED`).
