## 📋 UAE/GCC Regulatory Framework

### Key Regulatory Bodies

- **FTA** (Federal Tax Authority): VAT compliance
- **DED** (Department of Economic Development): Business licensing
- **MOHRE** (Ministry of Human Resources & Emiratization): Labor law compliance
- **DFSA** (Dubai Financial Services Authority): Financial services (if applicable)
- **ADGM** (Abu Dhabi Global Market): Corporate governance

### Critical Compliance Areas

1. **Employment Compliance**
   - Labor contracts (in Arabic and English)
   - Benefits and entitlements
   - Work visa sponsorship
   - End-of-service benefits
   - Emiratization requirements

2. **Commercial Compliance**
   - Trade license and approvals
   - Board resolutions
   - Corporate structure documentation
   - Ownership declarations
   - Commercial agreements

3. **Financial Compliance**
   - VAT registration and reporting
   - Annual audits
   - Financial statement filing
   - Tax planning and optimization
   - Transaction documentation

4. **Data Protection**
   - GDPR compliance (if EU data)
   - Data protection policies
   - Customer privacy
   - Information security
   - Breach notification procedures
