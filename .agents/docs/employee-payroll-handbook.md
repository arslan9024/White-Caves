## 💳 2. AED Bank Payout & Commission Structure

### Payout Disbursement Logic
1. **Gross Revenue Ingested**: Sale or tenancy deal funds cleared in escrow or client account.
2. **VAT 5% FTA Deduction**: 5% UAE Federal Tax Authority VAT computed and assigned to tax bucket.
3. **Company Reserve (30%)**: 30% retained for operations, portal listing syndication, and office overheads.
4. **Agent Net Commission (70%)**: 70% credited directly to the closing broker's monthly payroll sheet.
5. **WPS / Direct IBAN Transfer**: Net salary + commissions disbursed via UAE Wage Protection System (WPS) or direct IBAN transfer to Emirates NBD / FAB.

### Sample Payout Calculation

```
Property Sale Price:           AED 4,000,000
Gross Commission (2%):         AED    80,000
UAE VAT 5% (on commission):    AED     4,000
Total Cleared Invoice:         AED    84,000

Company Share (30%):           AED    24,000
Agent Net Commission (70%):    AED    56,000

Agent Monthly Base Salary:     AED    12,000
Total Net Monthly Transfer:    AED    68,000 → Credited to Agent IBAN
```

## 🏆 3. Dual Employee of the Year Competitions

White Caves hosts two independent, real-time competition pools to celebrate top-tier performance without cross-department bias.

### Pool A: 🏬 Leasing Agent of the Year (30 Agents)

- **Target Audience**: Residential & Commercial Leasing Brokers (`LEAS-01` through `LEAS-30`).
- **Scoring Engine Matrix**:

$$\text{Leasing Score} = (0.35 \times \text{Contracts Signed}) + (0.30 \times \text{AED Rent Volume}) + (0.15 \times \text{Ejari SLA Speed}) + (0.20 \times \text{Tenant Rating})$$

- **Leaderboard Metrics**:
  1. Total Ejari Contracts Registered
  2. Total Gross Rental Volume (AED)
  3. Average Ejari Processing SLA (< 12 Hours Target)
  4. Tenant Satisfaction Score (5-Star Scale)
- **Grand Prize**: **Gold Trophy + AED 50,000 Cash Bonus + All-Inclusive Annual Luxury Trip to Maldives**.

### Pool B: 🏙️ Sales Agent of the Year (30 Agents)

- **Target Audience**: Secondary Market & Developer Off-Plan Specialists (`SALE-01` through `SALE-30`).
- **Scoring Engine Matrix**:

$$\text{Sales Score} = (0.40 \times \text{AED Sales Revenue}) + (0.25 \times \text{HNW Deals} \ge 5\text{M}) + (0.20 \times \text{Form 6 Conversion}) + (0.15 \times \text{Oqood SLA})$$

- **Leaderboard Metrics**:
  1. Total Gross Commission Generated (AED)
  2. Count of High-Net-Worth Transactions (≥ AED 5,000,000)
  3. Form F / Form 6 Conversion Efficiency
  4. Oqood Off-Plan Registration Speed
- **Grand Prize**: **Platinum Trophy + Managing Director Rolex Submariner + 10% Annual Company Profit Share**.
