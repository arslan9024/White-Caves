# Dubai DLD & Ejari Compliance Protocol
Use this skill whenever working on Tenancy, Leasing, or Property modules.
1. **Ejari Requirement**: All rental contracts must generate an Ejari ID.
2. **PDC (Post-Dated Cheques)**: Cheques must be validated (1, 2, 4, or 6 cheques standard).
3. **VAT**: UAE statutory VAT is 5% on commercial properties, 0% (exempt) on residential.
4. **Form 12**: Legal eviction notices require exactly 12 months' notice via Notary Public.
