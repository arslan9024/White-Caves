# White Caves Real Estate LLC — Project Vision Manifest

## Executive Overview

White Caves Real Estate LLC operates as a premier luxury real estate brokerage, property management, and investment firm in Dubai, UAE. This **Project Vision Manifest** establishes the foundational architectural goals, business imperatives, and functional scope for the White Caves Sovereign CRM Cockpit and Web Platform.

---

## 🎨 Sovereign Brand Palette & Visual Identity

The platform strictly enforces the White Caves Brand Identity across all user interfaces, components, and media:

- **Primary Red (`#EF4444`)**: Used exclusively for primary call-to-action buttons, key status metrics, active navigation states, and map marker pins.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure background panels, modal surfaces, card containers, and primary text highlights.
- **Slate Text (`#1E293B`)**: Ultra-clean dark typography, layout borders, and table headers.

## 🏛️ RUP 4-Tier Documentation Architecture

Our engineering workflow follows the **Rational Unified Process (RUP)** framework structured into four specialized domain subdirectories:

1. **`01_requirements_engineering/`**: Functional Specifications, Software Requirements Specifications (SRS), and Requirements Evolution Change Logs.
2. **`02_software_design/`**: Software Design Documents (SDD), Database Architecture, RBAC State Gating, and Tech Replacement Rules.
3. **`03_use_cases/`**: Operational Use Case specifications including Lead Distribution SLAs and MD Impersonation Matrices.
4. **`04_flowcharts/`**: Interactive ASCII Navigation Maps and workflow state stepper diagrams.

## 📊 Core Business Objectives & System Specifications

1. **9,378-Unit Managed Inventory**: Centralized data management for Dubai master communities including DAMAC Hills 2, Palm Jumeirah, Downtown Dubai, and Dubai Marina.
2. **1-12-108 Organizational Structure**: Single-pane management layout encompassing 12 specialized departments, 12 Level-4 Managers, and 108 Level-3 Squad Supervisors.
3. **15-Minute Lead Ingestion SLA**: Automated portal ingestion (Bayut, Property Finder, Dubizzle) with instant AI lead scoring, round-robin agent assignment, and Nadia WhatsApp acknowledgment.
4. **Form 12 Ejari Eviction & PDC Vault**: Complete compliance suite for statutory 12-month eviction notices, Ejari renewals, and Post-Dated Cheque (PDC) vault management.
5. **UAE FTA VAT 5% Compliance**: Automated multi-currency AED accounting engine supporting VAT 5% tax invoice formatting, commission splits, and close-month ledger freezes.
