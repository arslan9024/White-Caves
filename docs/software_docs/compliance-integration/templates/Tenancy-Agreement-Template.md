# Tenancy Agreement Template Notes

This document provides an internal drafting outline for a UAE/Dubai tenancy agreement. It is not legal advice and must be reviewed by qualified counsel against current RERA, DLD, and landlord-tenant requirements before use.

## Recommended Sections

1. Parties and legal capacity
2. Property details and unit description
3. Term, commencement date, and renewal conditions
4. Rent amount, payment schedule, and late-payment rules
5. Deposit, post-dated cheques, and refund terms
6. Maintenance responsibilities and repair escalation
7. Notice, termination, and non-renewal rules
8. Signatures, witnesses, and annexes

## Required drafting fields

- landlordName
- tenantName
- propertyAddress
- leaseStartDate
- leaseEndDate
- monthlyRentAED
- securityDepositAED
- paymentSchedule
- noticePeriodDays
- authorizedSignatories

## Review checklist

- Confirm RERA and Ejari requirements
- Verify deposit, rent increase, and notice clauses
- Confirm PDC handling and breach provisions
- Attach any addendum or annexes before execution
