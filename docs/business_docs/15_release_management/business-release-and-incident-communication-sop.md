# Business Release and Incident Communication SOP

**Status:** Active  
**Owner:** Executive Operations / Compliance Lead  
**Review Cadence:** Quarterly and after major incidents

## Purpose

This SOP defines how White Caves communicates release changes, service incidents, and material operational impacts to internal stakeholders and external parties where required.

## Communication Triggers

- New release or rollout to production
- Major service degradation or outage
- Data integrity issue or compliance-sensitive incident
- Customer-impacting change in availability, pricing, or access

## Communication Workflow

1. Incident or release owner confirms the issue, impact, and timing.
2. Executive operations and compliance review the message for accuracy and sensitivity.
3. Internal stakeholders receive a brief status update with owner, impact, and next update time.
4. External or customer-facing communication is issued only where required and approved.

## Required Message Elements

- summary of the event or release
- impact on customers, agents, or operations
- owner and escalation contact
- current mitigation or rollback status
- next update time

## Control Expectations

- High-impact incidents must receive a first update within 30 minutes.
- Release communications must be issued before any material launch window begins.
- All communications should be logged for future reference and audit review.
