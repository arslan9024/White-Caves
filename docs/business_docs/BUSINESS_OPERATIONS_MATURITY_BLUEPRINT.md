# Business Operations Maturity Blueprint

## Purpose

This document defines the business operating maturity model for White Caves so that product, operations, compliance, and delivery work remain tightly aligned.

## Business operating layers

1. Leadership and governance
   - Clarify strategic direction, accountability, and escalation paths.

2. Product and delivery alignment
   - Connect feature intent to customer outcomes, acceptance criteria, and release impact.

3. Compliance and risk control
   - Ensure regulatory, privacy, and policy obligations are visible and testable.

4. Operational excellence
   - Standardize workflows, handoffs, and quality expectations across departments.

## How to use this blueprint

- Adopt it as a reference when creating or updating business process documents.
- Use it to ensure that each feature or workflow includes ownership, approval, outcomes, and success measurement.
- Pair it with the implementation traceability map for execution readiness.

## Maturity signals

A mature business operating layer should show:

- clear ownership;
- explicit decision rights;
- measurable outcomes;
- traceable downstream execution;
- auditable compliance and rollout controls.
