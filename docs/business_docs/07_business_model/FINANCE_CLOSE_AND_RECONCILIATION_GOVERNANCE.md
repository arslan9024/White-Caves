# Finance Close and Reconciliation Governance

**Status:** Active  
**Owner:** Finance Lead / Accounts Controller  
**Review Cadence:** Monthly

## Purpose

This document establishes the monthly close cadence, reconciliation checkpoints, and approval matrix for finance and accounts operations.

## Monthly Close Calendar

- Day 1–3: collect transaction data and confirm bank activity
- Day 4–5: reconcile property-related receipts, commissions, and disbursements
- Day 6–7: review receivables, payables, and tax-related entries
- Day 8: management review and sign-off

## Reconciliation Checkpoints

- Bank statements vs ledger
- Commission payable vs settlement records
- Property management receipts vs invoices
- VAT input/output summaries vs filing support schedules
- Payroll and staff reimbursement entries

## Approval Matrix

- Routine posting: Finance Analyst
- Review of reconciliations: Accounts Controller
- Material variance or exception: Finance Lead + Executive Office
- Final month close sign-off: Finance Lead

## Control Expectations

- All reconciliations must be dated and signed-off by the responsible owner.
- Material variances must be investigated within 3 business days.
- Open items older than 7 business days must be escalated.

## KPI and Reporting Targets

- Monthly close completed by day 8: 100% target
- Reconciliation aging under 7 days: 95%+ target
- Exception resolution time: under 3 business days
