## 🏢 CORE BUSINESS MODEL

### Revenue Streams

#### 1. **Commission on Sales** (45% of revenue)
- **Structure**: 2-3% of sale price (buyer + seller commission split)
- **Average Transaction**: AED 1,000,000 (~$272K USD)
- **Commission per transaction**: AED 20,000-30,000 (~$5.5K-8K USD)
- **Volume Target**: 1,500+ transactions/year
- **Annual Revenue**: AED 30M - 45M (~$8.2M - $12.2M USD)
- **Example**: 
  - Sale price: AED 1M
  - Commission rate: 2.5%
  - Gross commission: AED 25,000
  - Broker share: AED 12,500 (50%)
  - Agent share: AED 12,500 (50%)

#### 2. **Commission on Leases** (25% of revenue)
- **Structure**: 5-10% of 1st year annual rent (landlord + tenant)
- **Average Monthly Rent**: AED 5,000 (for 1-BR in Dubai)
- **Annual Rent**: AED 60,000
- **Commission per transaction**: AED 3,000-6,000
- **Volume Target**: 800+ lease transactions/year
- **Annual Revenue**: AED 2.4M - 4.8M (~$650K - $1.3M USD)

#### 3. **Property Management Services** (15% of revenue)
- **Service**: Ongoing property management (for landlords)
- **Fee Structure**: 6-8% of monthly rent collected
- **Average Monthly Rent**: AED 5,000
- **Monthly Fee**: AED 300-400
- **Annual Fee per Property**: AED 3,600 - 4,800
- **Portfolio Target**: 500+ properties under management
- **Annual Revenue**: AED 1.8M - 2.4M (~$490K - $650K USD)

#### 4. **Premium Listings & Advertising** (10% of revenue)
- **Service**: Featured listings, premium placement, ads on web/WhatsApp
- **Premium Listing Fee**: AED 2,000 - 5,000 per property (one-time, annual renewal)
- **Commission**: Let's assume 20% of listings upgraded to premium tier (~1,876 properties)
- **Annual Revenue**: AED 3.75M - 9.4M (~$1M - 2.5M USD)

#### 5. **Interior Design & Furnishing** (5% of revenue)
- **Service**: Interior design services, furniture rental/sale for properties
- **Average Project**: AED 20,000 - 50,000
- **Volume Target**: 50-100 projects/year
- **Annual Revenue**: AED 1M - 5M (~$270K - 1.3M USD)

### Total Annual Revenue (Projected)
- **Conservative**: AED 39M - 67M (~$10.6M - $18.2M USD)
- **Optimistic**: AED 50M - 100M (~$13.6M - $27.2M USD)
