# White Caves Business Operating Manual

**Status:** Active  
**Version:** 2026-08-03  
**Purpose:** Consolidate the company operating model, department responsibilities, workflow expectations, and automation opportunities into one executive-ready business reference.

---

## 1) Business model at a glance

White Caves Real Estate LLC operates as a Dubai-native brokerage and property operations platform with a strong compliance, digital workflow, and AI-assisted service model. The business combines:

- sales and leasing execution;
- property operations and handover management;
- investor and off-plan support;
- finance and commission governance;
- regulated communications and customer service;
- AI-assisted productivity with human oversight.

The operating model is designed to support fast response times, transparent financial controls, and consistent customer experience across both frontline staff and back-office functions.

## 3) Operating rhythm

### Daily operating cadence

- Morning review of leads, viewings, maintenance items, and exceptions.
- Midday handling of urgent customer and operational escalations.
- Afternoon reporting on finance, pipeline, and compliance follow-ups.

### Weekly operating cadence

- Sales and leasing pipeline review.
- Inventory quality and compliance review.
- Finance reconciliation and payout review.
- Automation and support workflow review.

### Monthly and quarterly cadence

- KPI dashboard review.
- Market and regulatory review.
- Service quality and process improvement review.
- Release and incident communication review where applicable.

## 5) Department-level operating expectations

### Executive & Strategy

- Maintain company priorities and growth roadmap.
- Review business health using KPI dashboards.
- Approve major process or system changes.

### Sales & Leasing

- Maintain clean lead and deal stages.
- Respond to inquiries consistently and fast.
- Ensure documentation and follow-up are complete.

### Property Operations

- Keep listings and inventory accurate.
- Resolve tenant/landlord requests with clear ownership.
- Preserve audit trail for changes and actions.

### Finance & Commission

- Keep all financial records tied to operational events.
- Review variances and exceptions promptly.
- Preserve reporting accuracy for management and compliance review.

### Marketing & Communications

- Build campaigns around clear audience segments.
- Ensure messaging and follow-up are consistent.
- Track conversion outcomes from each campaign.

### Compliance & Legal

- Operate within UAE and Dubai regulatory expectations.
- Maintain evidence, approvals, and retention controls.
- Escalate legal or compliance exceptions quickly.

### HR & People Operations

- Keep onboarding, access, and workforce documents current.
- Support role clarity and performance management.
- Preserve privacy and security controls for staff records.

### Technology & Product

- Deliver stable workflows, secure integrations, and measurable improvements.
- Preserve documentation and release discipline.
- Support AI assistant adoption with clear operational governance.

## 7) Governance and KPI expectations

Key management indicators should include:

- lead response time;
- viewing-to-offer conversion rate;
- maintenance turnaround time;
- finance reconciliation timeliness;
- compliance exception count;
- onboarding completion time;
- campaign conversion rate;
- assistant adoption and resolution quality.

These metrics should be reviewed regularly and tied back to the documentation and implementation backlog.
