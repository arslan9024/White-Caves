# Business Docs Upgrade Roadmap — 2026 Q3

**Purpose:** Keep White Caves business documentation continuously current and execution-ready for planning waves.

## Workstreams

| ID    | Workstream                            | Owner Lane                   | Deliverable                                                                    | Due Window |

| BD-01 | Company profile normalization         | Executive + Compliance       | Canonical profile page with verified IDs, authorities, renewal calendars       | Week 1     |

---

## Evidence Standards

Each completed workstream must include:

1. Business rule updates
2. Ownership and review cadence
3. Operational metrics/KPIs
4. Compliance references (RERA/DLD/PDPL/AML where applicable)
5. Explicit dependencies into `docs/plans/waves/*`

---

## Quarterly Review Cadence

- **Monthly:** operational accuracy review (inventory, finance close, SLA performance)
- **Quarterly:** strategic update (Dubai market trends, regulation deltas, service model shifts)
- **Semi-Annual:** full policy reconciliation (HR, legal, data governance, incident response)
