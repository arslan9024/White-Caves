# Quarterly Market and Regulatory Review

**Status:** Active  
**Owner:** Intelligence Lead / Compliance Lead  
**Review Cadence:** Quarterly

## Purpose

This document defines the cadence for reviewing Dubai market conditions, regulation changes, and operational implications for White Caves. It is intended to keep product, operations, and compliance teams aligned before major planning waves.

## Review Inputs

- Dubai property demand and pricing movement
- Rental index and renewal pressure signals
- RERA, DLD, AML, and PDPL updates
- Channel mix changes and lead acquisition efficiency
- Internal KPI shifts in conversion, inventory accuracy, and service quality

## Review Cadence

- Week 1: gather inputs from market intelligence, finance, and compliance teams
- Week 2: summarize the quarter’s changes and consequences
- Week 3: review the implications for product backlog and operating priorities
- Week 4: publish the final summary and assign follow-up owners

## Required Output

Each quarterly review should include:

- key market changes
- key regulatory changes
- risk or opportunity summary
- recommended action items
- owners and due dates

## KPI and Control Expectations

- Review completed by the end of each quarter: 100% target
- Action items with owners and due dates: 100% target
- High-risk regulatory changes escalated within 48 hours: 100% target
