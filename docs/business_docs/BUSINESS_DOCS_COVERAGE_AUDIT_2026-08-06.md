# Business Docs Coverage Audit — 2026-08-06

## 1) Executive verdict

White Caves already has strong business documentation coverage across company profile, governance, workflows, compliance, CRM features, and release operations. The main gap is not absence of content, but completeness of the numbered coverage chain and the consistency of links, owners, and evidence expectations across domains.

## 3) What is already covered well

1. Executive operating context and business baseline.
2. Department and role structure.
3. Core workflows and business operating patterns.
4. Compliance, legal, and policy intent.
5. CRM feature narrative and business-facing requirements.
6. Release and operations governance structure.

## 4) Improvement priorities

1. Create one canonical HR policy entrypoint and route all HR references there.
2. Standardize all company credential references to one canonical documents path.
3. Add explicit owners and review cadence for quarterly market and compliance reviews.
4. Strengthen the numbered traceability path from business docs to software docs to plans and tests.
5. Expand the scenario library from seed-stage coverage to a phased implementation roadmap.

## 5) Recommended next step

Treat this audit as the business-side coverage baseline and then align it to the software and planning traceability pack so every major capability has a visible path from business intent to implementation evidence.
