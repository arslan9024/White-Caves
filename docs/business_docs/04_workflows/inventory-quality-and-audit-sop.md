# Inventory Quality and Monthly Audit SOP

**Status:** Active  
**Owner:** Operations Lead / Inventory Manager  
**Review Cadence:** Monthly

## Purpose

This standard operating procedure defines how White Caves maintains inventory quality, performs monthly audits, and resolves data issues for properties, units, and availability records.

## Scope

This SOP applies to all property records that are listed, reserved, under offer, leased, or pending handover. It covers data completeness, duplicate detection, availability accuracy, and ownership accountability.

## Monthly Audit Checklist

- Review new or changed property records for completeness.
- Validate unit status transitions against the current sales or leasing pipeline.
- Check mandatory fields such as title deed, pricing, location, and photo coverage.
- Investigate duplicate, stale, or conflicting records.
- Confirm that follow-up actions are routed to the right owner.

## KPI and Control Expectations

- Inventory record completeness target: 98%+
- Duplicate records detected and resolved monthly: 100% of identified cases
- Stale active listings reviewed monthly: 100%
- Data quality exception age: target under 7 days

## Escalation Rules

- Any critical discrepancy affecting availability, pricing, or compliance must be escalated within 24 hours.
- Repeated issues across the same property source should be routed to the operations lead for process remediation.

## Compliance and Reporting Notes

- Inventory records should support downstream compliance, finance, and customer-facing workflows.
- Audit outcomes should be summarized in the monthly operations review and linked to any remediation backlog.
