# AI Automation and Assistant Map

**Status:** Active  
**Version:** 2026-08-03  
**Purpose:** Translate business functions into practical AI assistant, workflow automation, and system integration opportunities for White Caves.

---

## 1) Automation philosophy

White Caves should use AI assistants to accelerate repetitive, high-volume, or time-sensitive work while preserving human control for approvals, negotiations, legal actions, finance decisions, and strategic exceptions.

Automation should be applied in three layers:

1. Workflow automation — task routing, reminders, status transitions, notifications.
2. Assistant automation — AI support for drafting, summarizing, triage, and recommendations.
3. Decision support — analytics and scoring to improve prioritization and consistency.

## 3) Recommended assistant roles

### Lead handling assistant

- Qualifies incoming leads.
- Routes them to the right team and agent.
- Sends appropriate follow-up messages.
- Flags high-value or high-risk leads for human review.

### Operations assistant

- Tracks property and tenant issues.
- Summarizes open tasks and escalations.
- Suggests next actions based on workflow rules.

### Finance assistant

- Reviews payment, commission, and reconciliation exceptions.
- Produces summaries for management review.
- Highlights anomalies and overdue actions.

### Compliance assistant

- Tracks required actions for DLD, Ejari, AML, and PDPL controls.
- Warns about missing evidence or upcoming deadlines.
- Supports internal audit preparation.

### Marketing assistant

- Drafts campaign copy and segmentation logic.
- Suggests communications based on lead stage and interest.
- Tracks engagement and conversion signals.

### HR assistant

- Supports onboarding, policy reminders, and access review.
- Provides standard responses to common staff questions.
- Highlights missing documentation or policy actions.

---

## 4) Automation design principles

- Human approval remains mandatory for legal, finance, and compliance-critical actions.
- Every automated workflow should have a clear trigger, owner, and escalation path.
- Every assistant should be connected to a business rule or approval policy.
- Monitoring and audit logs should exist for all automation events.
- The system should provide clear fallback to human support when confidence is low.

---

## 5) Priority implementation sequence

1. Lead handling and follow-up automation.
2. Viewing scheduling and reminder automation.
3. Maintenance and operations triage automation.
4. Finance exception and reconciliation automation.
5. Compliance reminder and document control automation.
6. HR onboarding and access automation.
7. Executive reporting and KPI summarization automation.
