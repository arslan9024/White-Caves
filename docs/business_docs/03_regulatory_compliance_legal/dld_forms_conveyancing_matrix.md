## 1. Overview of Mandatory RERA Real Estate Forms

All property transactions in Dubai must strictly follow prescribed Dubai Land Department (DLD) and Real Estate Regulatory Agency (RERA) contract templates. Deviations or incorrect form citations invalidate marketing permits and expose the agency to regulatory fines.

│                    RERA MANDATORY CONTRACT PIPELINE                          │
├───────────────────┬───────────────────┬───────────────────┬─────────────────┤

## 2. Definitive RERA Forms Operational Matrix

### 2.1 Form A — Seller Marketing Agreement (Formerly Form 7)
- **Definition:** The legal contract signed between the Property Owner (Seller) and White Caves Real Estate LLC authorizing property marketing and listing representation.
- **Trakheesi Permit Requirement:** Every Form A generates a unique DLD Trakheesi advertising permit number. No public marketing (Property Finder, Bayut, Website, Social Media) can occur without an active Trakheesi permit linked to Form A.
- **Validity Limit:** Maximum 90 days per listing mandate. Non-exclusive mandates require separate Form A filings per agency.

### 2.2 Form B — Buyer Representation Agreement (Formerly Form 6)
- **Definition:** The official agreement signed between a prospective Property Purchaser (Buyer) and White Caves Real Estate LLC.
- **Scope:** Defines property requirements, budget parameters, broker commission split (standard 2% + 5% UAE VAT), and exclusivity duration.

### 2.3 Form I — Agent-to-Agent Co-Brokered Deal Agreement
- **Definition:** Mandatory co-brokerage agreement executed when two independent RERA-licensed agencies collaborate on a single secondary market transaction (e.g., Agency A represents Seller via Form A; White Caves represents Buyer via Form B).
- **Commission Lock:** Form I hardcodes commission split ratios (typically 50/50 of the total 2% buyer fee) before property viewings or offer submissions occur.

### 2.4 Form 7 — Official DLD Notice of Rent Increase
- **Legal Rule:** Under Law No. 33 of 2008, a Landlord wishing to increase Ejari rent upon lease renewal **must calculate the increase using the official RERA Rent Index Calculator** and serve written Form 7 notice to the Tenant **at least 90 days prior to the lease expiration date**.
- **Violation Consequence:** Notice served under 90 days renders rent increases legally void for the upcoming renewal term.

### 2.5 Form 12 — Official DLD Eviction Notice
- **Legal Rule:** Under Law No. 33 of 2008 (Article 25), a Landlord seeking tenant eviction for personal use, property sale, or major demolition **must serve Form 12 notice via Notary Public or Registered Mail**.
- **Mandatory Notification Window:** Requires a **strict 12-month notification timeline** calculated from the date of Notary Public service. (Serving 90-day eviction notices is legally invalid).

## 3. Conveyancing Execution Checklist

| Stage | Action Required | Responsible Party | Legal Form | SLA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Listing** | Verify Owner Title Deed & Emirates ID | Compliance Team | **Form A + Trakheesi** | < 2 Hours |
| **Buyer Onboarding** | Execute Buyer Mandate & KYC Check | Sales Broker | **Form B** | Prior to Viewings |
| **Co-Brokerage** | Sign Co-Broker Contract with Partner Agency | Sales Broker | **Form I** | Prior to Viewings |
| **Offer Acceptance** | Draft Unified Purchase Agreement | Conveyancer | **Form F (MOU)** | < 24 Hours |
| **Lease Renewal** | Calculate RERA Rent Index & Send Notice | Leasing Manager | **Form 7** | 90 Days Before Renewal |
| **Eviction Handling** | Serve Notary Public Eviction Notice | Legal Counsel | **Form 12** | 12 Months Before Date |
