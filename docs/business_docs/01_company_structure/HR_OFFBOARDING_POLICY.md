# HR Offboarding Policy

**Status:** Active  
**Owner:** People Operations / IT / Finance  
**Review Cadence:** Semi-annual

## Purpose

This policy ensures a controlled, compliant, and secure offboarding process for departing employees, contractors, and temporary staff.

## Offboarding Checklist

- Notify relevant managers and HR of resignation or termination.
- Confirm final working day and handover obligations.
- Revoke access to systems, email, CRM, and shared tools.
- Recover company assets such as laptops, keys, cards, and documents.
- Confirm final payment, benefits, and contractual obligations.
- Record the exit in the employee register and audit trail.

## Security Controls

- Access removal must be completed on or before the final day where possible.
- Sensitive files and internal communications must be returned or securely archived.
- Manager sign-off is required for asset return and handover completion.

## Compliance Expectations

- Exit records should remain available for audit and legal review.
- HR should coordinate with finance and compliance on final settlement checks.
- Any data-transfer or retention issue must be flagged to the compliance lead.

## KPI and Control Expectations

- Access removal completion rate: 100% before final day where feasible
- Asset return completion rate: 100%
- Offboarding case closure time: under 5 business days
