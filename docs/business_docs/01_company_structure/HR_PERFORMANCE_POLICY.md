# HR Performance Management Policy

**Status:** Active  
**Owner:** People Operations / Department Heads  
**Review Cadence:** Quarterly

## Purpose

This policy defines how employee performance is reviewed, documented, and improved across White Caves teams.

## Review Cycle

- Monthly check-ins for all staff
- Quarterly review for managers and team leads
- Annual review for all employees

## Performance Expectations

- Team members must meet role-based KPIs and behavioral expectations.
- Managers are responsible for documenting outcomes, coaching actions, and development needs.
- Performance concerns must be raised early and supported with evidence.

## Improvement Workflow

1. Manager identifies a performance gap or missed KPI.
2. Coaching discussion is held and documented.
3. A development plan is created with clear success milestones.
4. If performance does not improve, a formal Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) may be initiated.

## PIP Standard

- Duration: 30 to 60 calendar days
- Minimum review points: weekly or biweekly
- Required outcomes: measurable KPI improvement and documented support actions

## KPI and Control Expectations

- Quarterly review completion rate: 100%
- PIP completion rate: 100% of active PIPs reviewed on time
- Employee feedback response time: under 5 business days

## Compliance Notes

- Documentation must remain factual, timely, and consistent with local employment law.
- HR should keep review records for internal governance and audit readiness.
