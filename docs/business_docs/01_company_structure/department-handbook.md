## 01. Sales & Off-Plan Department

- **Headcount**: 30 Secondary Sales & Off-Plan Brokers
- **Mission**: Close high-value ready sales and developer off-plan reservations (Emaar, DAMAC, Sobha, Nakheel).
- **Core KPIs**: Total Gross Revenue (AED), High-Net-Worth Deal Volume (≥ AED 5M), Form 6 Conversion Rate.
- **Tools**: CRM Sales Kanban, Property Finder, Bayut, DLD REST Verification.

## 02. Property Inventory Department

- **Headcount**: 5 Listing Coordinators & Valuation Specialists
