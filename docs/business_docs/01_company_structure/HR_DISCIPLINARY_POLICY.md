# HR Disciplinary and Escalation Policy

**Status:** Active  
**Owner:** People Operations / HR Lead  
**Review Cadence:** Quarterly

## Purpose

This document sets a consistent, fair, and documented approach to disciplinary action, escalation, and case management.

## Policy Principles

- All issues must be handled consistently and with due process.
- Managers should address concerns promptly and document the facts clearly.
- Disciplinary severity should reflect the seriousness of the issue and prior history.

## Response Levels

- Informal coaching: minor performance or conduct concern
- Formal warning: repeated issue or moderate breach
- Final warning: serious misconduct or repeated non-compliance
- Termination review: severe misconduct, fraud, confidentiality breach, or repeated policy failure

## Escalation Path

1. Manager raises issue with HR.
2. HR reviews evidence and confirms the appropriate response level.
3. Leadership review is requested for serious cases.
4. Final action is documented and communicated to the employee.

## Recordkeeping Expectations

- Incident details, dates, witnesses, and actions must be retained.
- HR must preserve any supporting evidence and maintain confidentiality.
- Cases should be reviewed for pattern and repeated-risk exposure.

## KPI and Control Expectations

- Disciplinary case review turnaround: under 5 business days
- Documentation completeness rate: 100%
- Escalation for high-risk cases: immediate
