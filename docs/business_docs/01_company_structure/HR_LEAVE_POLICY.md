# HR Leave Policy

**Status:** Active  
**Owner:** People Operations / HR Lead  
**Review Cadence:** Quarterly and at policy change

## Purpose

This policy defines leave entitlements, approval workflow, and reporting expectations for White Caves employees and contractors working on Dubai-based operations.

## Leave Types

- Annual leave: 21 working days per full year for full-time employees
- Sick leave: up to 10 working days per calendar year with supporting evidence where required
- Emergency leave: up to 3 days per year, subject to manager approval
- Maternity/paternity leave: aligned to local employment law and internal HR approval
- Study or bereavement leave: reviewed case-by-case by People Operations

## Approval Flow

1. Employee submits leave request through HR workflow or manager channel.
2. Manager confirms workload coverage and approves or rejects within 2 business days.
3. HR records the leave request in the workforce tracker.
4. Payroll and operations teams are notified for coverage impact.

## Coverage and Service Impact

- Critical customer-facing teams must maintain minimum staffing coverage during peak periods.
- Leave approvals for revenue-critical roles require backup coverage confirmation.
- No leave request may be used to bypass compliance or client delivery obligations.

## KPI and Control Expectations

- Leave request response time: under 2 business days
- Leave utilization reporting: monthly to People Operations
- Unapproved absence rate: target below 2%

## Compliance Notes

- This policy must align with UAE labor obligations and any applicable employment contract terms.
- HR should maintain leave records securely and retain them according to the company retention schedule.
