### Regulatory Compliance Obligations

| Regulation | Authority | Key Obligation | Renewal / Frequency |
|------------|-----------|---------------|---------------------|
| Real Estate Brokers Registration | RERA / DLD | All agents hold valid RERA BRN | Annual |
| Anti-Money Laundering (AML) | UAE FIU / CBUAE | KYC/CDD on all transactions > AED 55,000 | Ongoing; SAR filed on suspicion |
| UAE Federal Law No. 20 of 2019 | CBUAE | AML/CFT program, enhanced due diligence for PEPs | Annual policy review |
| Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) — Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 | UAE TDRA | Consent, data minimization, breach notification within 72h | Ongoing |
| Ejari System | DLD / RERA | All tenancy contracts registered within 30 days of signing | Per contract |
| Oqood System | DLD | Off-plan unit sales registered before handover | Per transaction |
| VAT — Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 | UAE FTA | 5% VAT on commercial services; residential leases exempt | Quarterly FTA return |
| Real Estate Investment Funds | SCA | If managing pooled investments > AED 1M threshold | Annual |
| RERA Regulatory Form A / Form B | RERA | Signed by seller/buyer before marketing/sale | Per listing/transaction |
