## Why Two New Departments?

### 11 — Customer Experience
The existing departments handle lead capture and sales closure, but no department formally owns the **post-contact client journey**: VIP concierge, multilingual support, NPS measurement, and communication history. As White Caves scales to 200+ landlords and hundreds of buyers, a dedicated CX function becomes critical.

### 12 — Data & AI
White Caves has committed to 40 AI assistants. The **AI Engine group** (Quill, Lumen, Crest) and several Intelligence assistants require a centralised team to own model training, data pipelines, reporting infrastructure, and API integrations. This is distinct from the Technology department, which focuses on software engineering.
