In accordance with the Federal Decree Law No (20) of 2018 on Anti-Money Laundering and Combating the Financing of Terrorism (AML/CFT) and the Cabinet Decision No (10) of 2019 implementing said law, as well as the guidelines issued by the Department of Economic Development (DED) and the UAE Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU), White Caves Real Estate LLC hereby formally appoints you as the AML Compliance Officer for all AML/CFT matters.

This appointment is effective as of {{effectiveDate}} and will continue until further notice or until your resignation/termination from this position.

Scope of Responsibilities

As the AML Compliance Officer, you are authorized and responsible for the following:

  • Register and Maintain Company Profile on goAML Portal: Register and maintain White Caves Real Estate LLC's profile on the UAE FIU's goAML portal as the authorized point of contact for all AML/CFT communications.
  • Ensure Company-Wide AML/CFT Compliance: Ensure that all employees, agents, and representatives of White Caves adhere strictly to the AML/CFT policy and procedures established by the company.
  • Conduct Customer Due Diligence (CDD): Oversee and approve Customer Due Diligence and Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) for all customers, particularly for high-risk transactions exceeding AED 5,000,000 or involving Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs).
  • Monitor Transactions and Report Suspicious Activity: Monitor all property transactions for suspicious activity and file Suspicious Transaction Reports (STRs) with the UAE FIU via the goAML portal within the prescribed timeline.
  • Maintain Compliance Records: Maintain and organize all AML/CFT compliance records, including customer identification documents, CDD forms, transaction records, and training documentation, for a minimum of five (5) years.
  • Liaison with Regulatory Authorities: Represent White Caves Real Estate LLC in all AML/CFT-related communications with the UAE FIU, DED, and other regulatory authorities, including responding to inquiries and participating in compliance inspections.
  • Conduct Staff Training: Develop, deliver, and track annual mandatory AML/CFT training for all staff members, ensuring 100% participation and maintaining training records and certificates.
  • Update and Review Policies: Review the company's AML/CFT policies and procedures annually, update them as required by regulatory changes, and obtain Board approval for revisions.
  • Perform Risk Assessment: Assess and identify ML/FT risks specific to White Caves' real estate business and implement appropriate risk mitigation measures.
  • Conduct Internal Audits: Perform periodic internal audits to evaluate the effectiveness of the AML/CFT compliance program and recommend improvements.

AUTHORITY STATEMENT:

You are hereby authorized to represent White Caves Real Estate LLC in all AML/CFT-related matters and to execute all necessary acts and decisions required for the company's compliance with AML/CFT laws and regulations. Your authority includes, but is not limited to:

  • Filing Suspicious Transaction Reports (STRs) on the goAML portal
  • Approving Customer Due Diligence assessments and customer risk classifications
  • Conducting PEP screening and sanctions checks
  • Approving high-risk transactions
  • Conducting staff training and issuing certificates
  • Approving amendments to AML/CFT policies (subject to Board ratification)

This appointment confirms your authority and places the responsibility for AML/CFT compliance directly with you. Your diligence in this role is critical to ensuring that White Caves Real Estate LLC operates in full compliance with UAE AML/CFT laws and avoids serious regulatory penalties.
